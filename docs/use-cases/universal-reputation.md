# Universal Reputation

Plurality encompasses data from web2 and web3 sources to create a unified reputation tagged to a profile that could be used on any blockchain.

For example, Plurality's Gaming Profile can give gamers a way to aggregate all of their gaming credentials across every game they play, including both Web3 and Web2 games. This includes their usernames, their gaming histories and data, their achievements, the assets they own, their social connections and more.\
\
Gamers will be able to take their reputation and entire history with them on any game on any chain and even on supported games in web2. \
\
Similarly, reputation can be used as part of other profiles as well.&#x20;
