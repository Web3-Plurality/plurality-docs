# ❓ Why create an account on Plurality?

As a user, you might ask why you should be a part of the plurality network and create your personalized web3 account.&#x20;

Here are a few reasons why you should become a part of Plurality:

## Easy Account Login

Creating an account with Plurality is extremely easy. You can use your existing social accounts to create your web3 account. If you want to use MetaMask, that's also supported.&#x20;

## Social Recovery

You don't have to worry about losing your keys ever again. You need access to the social account with which you signed up and that will serve as your recovery mechanism.

## Personalized Experiences

You will be able to get better personalized experiences on various web3 dApps, without ever compromising your privacy.

## Sovereignty over your data

The data you link from web2 will be entirely under your control. Never will it ever again be used without your explicit consent.

## Data Monetization

Since data is truly yours, you will be able to demand incentivization if someone wants to use your data.

## Dataverse

You will become part of a data economy and a dataverse with many benefits yet to unfold.
