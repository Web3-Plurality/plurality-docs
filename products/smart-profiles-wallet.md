# Smart Profiles Wallet

Smart Profiles Wallet is the embedded profiles wallet that any dApp can integrate to allow sign in with profile functionality for their users.

{% hint style="info" %}
Go to [guides](../smart-profiles/developer-guides/embedded-login/) to learn about how to integrate it with your dApp
{% endhint %}

dApps can register with Plurality and configure which profile do they want their users to login with (examples: Social, Metaverse, Gaming)

When users login to this dApp, they will be shown a specific profile to login with. By connecting their profile, they allow to share some insights about their interests and preferences (in a privacy-preserving way).

dApps can use these information to tailor experiences according to this user, thereby providing users with a better onboarding and overall experience on the dApp.
