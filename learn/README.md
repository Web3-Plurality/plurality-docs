# 👋 Plurality

> Plurality solves the user retention problem of web3 by letting users easily onboard to web3 platforms along with their personality characteristics (reputation and interests) by linking, verifying, and aggregating data from their existing social profiles in a privacy-preserving way.
>
> Plurality is more than just an easy login. It lets users get personalized experience on dApps while staying in full control of their data.
