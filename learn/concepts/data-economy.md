# 🤑 Data Economy

## User-centric data economy

If users get true data sovereignty, then it is also possible to create a user-centric data economy, which is one of the goals of Plurality.

Data economy means that the users' data can be used for several purposes by the user if they want and only for the data they accept. The consumers of this data can incentivize users to open their data, and this incentivization may lead to monetization opportunities for the user.

### Data consumers

There can be many different consumers of user data, each utilizing the data for various purposes. It is worth noting that even today, on web2, user data is indexed, profiled, and used in many different ways. However, most of these uses are transparent to the user, resulting in no tangible benefit other than a better experience.&#x20;

The following are some examples of consumers of such data:

#### LLM Training Data

With the recent expansion of AI tools, the need for data for training has increased exponentially. Until now, public user data has been used for training these tools, resulting in legal battles over copyright issues and ownership of data.&#x20;

A better way to approach this problem is by enabling users to allow for their data to be used for training purposes if they want and get appropriately incentivized for it as well.&#x20;

#### Advertisements

Advertisements have been a method to reach the target audience for centuries. However, in the last few decades, the profiling of users for advertisements has happened without their consent and without them knowing what data is being used.&#x20;

However, advertisement, when used correctly, also benefits users in letting them know of potential opportunities about things they are interested in e.g., discounted tickets for their favorite band, pink shoes that they have been looking for, etc.&#x20;

A more empowered way of viewing advertisements would be to enable users to allow their (certain) data to be used for advertisement profiling. In this way, they would have the option to ensure that only those of their data points are being used that they are comfortable with.

#### Recommendation engines

Every person that lands on the internet wants to consume data relevant to their interests. Recommendation engines could use user interest data to show personalized content if the user allows it.&#x20;

#### Scientific or Social Research

User data could also be used for scientific or social research purposes. User can choose to support specific research by allowing the consumption of their data transparently.
