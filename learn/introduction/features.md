# ✨ Features

Plurality’s features offer benefits for all participants of the network.

## Easy onboarding for users

Plurality offers users the ability to create a web3 account using their social logins. No more handling seed phrases or dealing with complicated workflows. Sign in with your social logins just like on any web2 platform.

## Personalization of web3 account

Plurality allows users to import a subset of their data from web2 platforms and link it with their web3 accounts. By linking this data, users attach aspects of their personality (reputation and interests) with their account and use them in relevant contexts on different dApps. This allows for a web3 account to act more than just a wallet and allows for wallet personalization.&#x20;

## Personalization of experience on dApps

Plurality enables dApps to get access to certain user data based on context and provide a personalized experience using this data.

DApps can request for specific content from certain web2 profiles by appropriately parametrizing the Plurality's widget.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
To see how to integrate the Plurality widget in any dApp, visit the [developer docs.](https://app.gitbook.com/s/gZBmCOfZXVqw58yDkvWu/quickstart/build-your-first-dapp-with-plurality)
{% endhint %}

## Data Sovereignty

Plurality allows users to gain control of their data and utilize their data the way they want. Users control when and how their data is used on web3 dApps. Moreover, they can also monetize this data if they want.

{% hint style="info" %}
To see it in action, create your web3 account by visiting the [dashboard](https://app.plurality.network)
{% endhint %}
