# 👷 Relevant jobs on gig platforms

One thing web3 has been successful at is creating a unified payment system for the whole world. And with this comes the option of employing people from any part of the world.&#x20;

Web3-based gig platforms have a great chance to become ubiquitous soon. However, such platforms require access to the qualifications or skillset of the person offering their services. Since a resume rarely shows the exact caliber of a person, therefore, more and more employers now opt to ask for a portfolio or try to see online what the person has done.

In the web3 world where the user is just a wallet, knowing this was not possible before Plurality.&#x20;

Every person on a gig platform who needs a way to showcase their skill set via their portfolio on web2 platforms like GitHub, Behance, Stackoverflow, etc, they can do so now via Plurality.

The gig platforms can customize their listings with relevant gigs or employers.

{% hint style="info" %}
Example platforms where data for this feature could be fetched: LinkedIn, GitHub, Stackoverflow, Reddit, Medium, Behance, Vercel etc.
{% endhint %}
