# 🎭 Social feed personalization

Every social platform involves a group of people looking to connect with interesting communities and content. Some examples of social platforms are:

* **Facebook/Instagram:** People want to view content from their friends and topics/influencers they follow. They also want to join groups of people with similar interests.
* **YouTube:** People want to watch videos of their interest
* **SoundCloud:** People want to listen to music of the genres/artists they like.

In all these platforms, one thing is constant: _Personalized Social Feed._ When a user lands on these platforms, the feed is personalized according to their interests.

This feature is lacking in web3 platforms. With plurality, dApps can get access to the interest data from various platforms and customize their feed accordingly, all without invading user's privacy.

{% hint style="info" %}
Example platforms where data for this feature could be fetched: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, SoundCloud, Reddit, etc.
{% endhint %}
