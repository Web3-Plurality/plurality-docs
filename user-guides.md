# User Guides

As a user, you can use OCL in the following ways:

## ChatWYC Portal

ChatWYC (With Your Context) lets you create and manage your contextual smart profiles, and chat with different LLMs with injected context of your choice. (Coming soon)

## OpenContext Extension

Keep collecting your context as you browse and use chat applications like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini. Your context, once collected, becomes reusable across other LLMs. (Coming soon)

## Native App Integrations

Use apps in our ecosystem that use our Context SDK or "Sign in with Plurality" to inject context automatically
